Product Lead

Livingthings AI

As Product Lead I was responsible for turning concepts into hardware that could actually be built — devices that were usable, reliable and manufacturable at small scale.

The work ran end to end: mechanical design in SolidWorks, designing and iterating custom enclosures around the electronics, then working alongside the electrical engineering team so PCB layout, thermal behaviour, port alignment and assembly all resolved together rather than fighting each other.

What I did

  • Led mechanical design and prototyping of the product housing, from concept through to pre-production.
  • Used FDM and SLA 3D printing for rapid iteration and functional testing.
  • Collaborated on PCB layout for fit, port alignment and thermal management inside the enclosure.
  • Managed component sourcing and built relationships with manufacturers for small-batch production runs.
