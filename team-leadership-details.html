BTech Mechanical Engineering · 2020 — 2024

IIT Bombay

I read Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, graduating in 2024. Two threads ran through those four years: research in the lab, and building teams outside it.

Research

I worked on femtosecond-laser optics — ultrafast pulses on the order of a quadrillionth of a second, used to machine and measure at scales conventional tooling cannot reach. Alongside that I worked on terrain-adapting hexapod robots: six-legged platforms that adjust their gait to ground they have not seen before.

Both are direct ancestors of what Bortex builds now — precision optics and machines that have to work without being told what they will find.

Team Rakshak & Inter-IIT

I served as VP of Marketing and Head of Design, owning the team's brand identity and the money that kept it running.

  • Built and ran the marketing strategy that attracted sponsors and grew a following.
  • Designed team apparel, brand guidelines and sponsorship proposals that converted into partnerships.
  • Managed a small team of designers and content creators.
  • Worked with technical leads so the branding matched what the team was actually building.
