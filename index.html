01 — About

Hardware, autonomy, and the gap between seeing and deciding.

Shubham Dutta is the founder and CEO of Bortex, a defence-technology company headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Bortex builds AI-powered border observability and tactical awareness systems for the Indian Armed Forces.

He studied Mechanical Engineering at IIT Bombay, graduating in 2024, where his work spanned femtosecond-laser optics research and terrain-adapting hexapod robots. Before founding Bortex he co-founded Vibe, an agentic-commerce startup, led product at Livingthings AI, worked as an APM and reliability engineer at Paar Autonomy, and worked on AI supply chain at Zomato.

Bortex was incorporated in June 2026 and incubated in July 2026 at ITI Limited, a Government of India enterprise, through the Vinyas Startup Hub programme.

06 — Questions

Frequently asked.

Who is Shubham Dutta?

Shubham Dutta is the founder and CEO of Bortex, a defence-technology company based in Bengaluru, India. He holds a BTech in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Bombay and previously worked at Vibe, Livingthings AI, Paar Autonomy and Zomato.

What is Bortex?

Bortex is an Indian defence-technology company founded in June 2026 and headquartered in Bengaluru. It builds AI-powered border observability and tactical awareness systems for the Indian Armed Forces, built on its X-Matrix sensor-fusion architecture.

What products does Bortex build?

Bortex builds four systems: Vanguard, soldier-mounted augmented-reality glasses; Perimeter, autonomous border-surveillance towers; Trident, an AI counter-drone platform; and Vigil, a multi-domain air, sea and ground robotics mesh.

Where is Bortex based?

Bortex is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. In July 2026 it was incubated at ITI Limited, a Government of India enterprise, through the Vinyas Startup Hub programme.

What is the X-Matrix architecture?

X-Matrix is the sensor-fusion backbone built by Bortex. It ingests data from cameras, radar, acoustic sensors and unmanned assets in real time and fuses it into one operating picture across air, land and sea.

What did Shubham Dutta do before founding Bortex?

Before Bortex, Shubham Dutta co-founded Vibe, an agentic-commerce startup, led product at Livingthings AI, worked as an APM and reliability engineer at Paar Autonomy, worked on AI supply chain at Zomato, and researched femtosecond-laser optics at IIT Bombay.
